System Guide: How to Test & What to Look For

@AppleSupport Grounded AI Support Agent • Evaluation Audit

1. What is this system?

This system is an enterprise AI support agent built on the Twitter Customer Support dataset (2.81M tweets), specifically for @AppleSupport. The agent does not simply generate arbitrary replies from general knowledge—it strictly grounds every response in 4,800 historical Apple resolution dialogues and enforces deterministic safety guardrails.

2. What to do (How to test the system live):

  • Test a Diagnostic Inquiry: Type an inquiry like "My battery is draining fast after the iOS 11 update". Notice the agent predicts BATTERY_POWER_CHARGING, selects AUTO_HANDLE, and provides standard diagnostic steps (Settings → Battery Health).
  • Test a Safety Escalation: Type "I dropped my phone on concrete and shattered the screen". Notice the agent detects physical damage and immediately selects ESCALATE TO HUMAN with an in-person Genius Bar appointment recommendation.
  • Test an Account Security Trigger: Type "Someone locked me out of my Apple ID and changed my recovery email". Notice the immediate escalation with account security routing.
  • Inspect Retrieved Historical Evidence: On any agent reply, click the "View Grounding Evidence" accordion to view the exact historical Apple tweets and cosine similarity scores.

3. Understanding the Outputs & Guardrails

AUTO_HANDLE

Granted when top vector similarity is ≥ 0.38 and no safety triggers (physical damage, account hijacking, legal threats) are detected.

ESCALATE TO HUMAN

Triggered immediately if similarity is < 0.38 (ungrounded cold start) or if safety-critical keywords are found. Yields 100% recall on required escalations.
